I loved this book with all of my heart. Resonated so hard with all of the characters - yes, even the 65 year old retiree - and somehow, left feeling inspired after each chapter. Librarian Sayuchi said it best, *"Readers make their own personal connections to the words, perspective of the writer's intentions, and each reader gains something unique."*

I gained an appreciation of the mundane. Of being satisfied with where I am, with who I am, and with what I am in life. And I'm excited to see what comes next - maybe felting frogs is in my future?
